🚂 Platform 9¾ — Warm-up & Golden Rules

⏱ 8 min · Objetivo: baixar a guarda, ativar o inglês e combinar as regras da aula.

⭐ The Golden Rules

1. Message first, grammar second.
2. Mistakes are magic: they help us learn. 🪄
3. If you don’t know a word, explain it!
4. Think out loud. Silence is okay, but try! 🤫

🧠 Magic phrases (keep them on the table!)

Let me think…I think…I mean…It’s similar to…It’s something that…I don’t remember the word, but…How can I explain this?

🧳 Pack your trunk ⏱ 5 min

🎯 Fluência: responder oralmente com respostas curtas e espontâneas.

Rayanne, answer out loud: Do you know Harry Potter? Who is your favourite character? What is your favourite magical thing? If you had a wand 🪄, what would you do first?

Expected: “My favourite is Hermione. I think she is… I mean… she is smart.” / “I would fly. It’s something that… I like.”

Aceite qualquer resposta, mesmo com erros. Anote 2–3 erros relevantes num papel (sem interromper) e use no “Feedback Time”. Se ela travar, ofereça a frase-chave (“Let me think…”) e espere 5 segundos antes de ajudar.

🧪 Potions Class — Comparative Adjectives

⏱ 22 min · Objetivo: comparar pessoas, lugares, objetos e criaturas do universo HP.

🔍 Notice first! ⏱ 2 min

Look at the pictures: 🐉 and 🦉. Which one is bigger? Say it in your own words. Then listen: “A dragon is bigger than an owl.” What changed? What word comes after?

Deixe ela formular a regra antes de você explicar (-er + than). Se ela responder “dragon big”, apenas repita corretamente: “Yes! The dragon is bigger than the owl.”

📖 The 6 spells of comparison

⚗️ Brew the right word ⏱ 5 min

🎯 Prática controlada. Rayanne diz a frase em voz alta e depois confere.

🪶 Translate the spell (PT → EN) ⏱ 6 min

🎯 Construir frases sozinha, da mais simples à mais complexa.

    Peça que diga em voz alta primeiro, depois escreva. Pode tentar de novo quantas vezes quiser. Gabarito: use o botão “🔮 Gabarito” no topo.

    🧙 Who is more…? ⏱ 5 min · Speaking

    🎯 Produzir comparativos próprios (fluência).

    Compare out loud (at least 2 sentences each): Harry 🆚 Ron · Hogwarts 🏰 🆚 your house 🏠 · A broomstick 🧹 🆚 a car 🚗 · A ghost 👻 🆚 a dragon 🐉 · Potions 🧪 🆚 Flying lessons 🧹.

    Expected: “Hogwarts is bigger than my house. I think it’s more beautiful.” / “A ghost is… I mean… less dangerous than a dragon.”

    Variação: “Who is more brave: Harry or Neville? Why?” Dê 20–30s por item. Meta: ela fala por períodos cada vez mais longos.

    🏰 The Great Hall — Speaking Adventures

    ⏱ 13 min · Escolha 3–4 desafios conforme a energia da aluna.

    🎩 Choose your Hogwarts house ⏱ 4 min

    🎯 Decidir e justificar com comparativos.

    The Sorting Hat is confused! Click a house, then tell me why. Use: “I think I’m more … than …”.

    Expected: “I choose Ravenclaw because I think I’m more… I mean, I like books.”

    🔮 Guess the character / Magical object challenge ⏱ 4 min

    🎯 Descrever sem dizer a palavra (circunlocução).

    Pick a secret card. Explain it without saying the word. Your teacher guesses!

    🧹 broomstick🧪 potion🎩 Sorting Hat🧙 Dumbledore🦉 Hedwig👻 Nearly Headless Nick
    Expected: “It’s something that you use to fly. It’s similar to a… I don’t remember the word, but you sit on it.”

    Troque de papel: depois a professora descreve e Rayanne adivinha (escuta). Se ela esquecer a palavra, celebre: “That’s exactly the skill!”

    🐉 Would you rather…? ⏱ 3 min

    🎯 Opinar, comparar e explicar motivos.

    Would you rather… have a pet owl 🦉 or a dragon 🐉? · fly on a broomstick 🧹 or be invisible 🧥? · live in a castle 🏰 or a forest 🌲? · be a witch/wizard or a Muggle?

    Expected: “I would rather have an owl because it’s cuter / easier than a dragon.”

    🪄 Mini roleplay: Ollivanders Wand Shop ⏱ 4 min

    🎯 Interação espontânea em situação real.

    You are a new student 🎒. The teacher is Mr Ollivander. Find your wand! Ask: “Can I try this wand?” “Is it bigger/better than…?” “How does it work?”

    Expected: “Hello! I need a wand, please.” / “This wand is longer than the other one.” / “I like it. I think it’s perfect.”

    Professora fala devagar, faz perguntas abertas (“What colour is it? Is it heavier than the first one?”). Não corrija durante o diálogo.

    🕯️ Feedback Time — The Golden Snitch of Mistakes ⏱ 3 min

    Aqui a professora apresenta o que anotou, sempre começando pelo que foi ótimo.

    🌟 Great!
    2 coisas que ela disse muito bem (frases ou estratégias).
    🔧 Fix it
    2–3 erros relevantes: ela mesma reformula (“Can you say it again?”).
    💡 New word
    1–2 palavras que ela precisou e não tinha.

    📜 Your Magical Homework

    Part 1: Flashcards · Part 2: Writing · Part 3: Speaking

    🃏 Part 1 — Vocabulary Flashcards

    Click to flip. Say the word, then the definition, then your own sentence! Press Hard (review tomorrow) or Easy (review in 3 days, then 7 days). Review Hard cards first. 🔁

    ✍️ Part 2 — Writing Challenge

    Write 6–8 sentences comparing characters, places or objects from Harry Potter. Use at least 5 comparative adjectives (e.g. taller, bigger, more dangerous, better). Don’t use a translator!

    Exemplo: “Hagrid is taller than Harry. A dragon is more dangerous than an owl. The library is quieter than the Great Hall. Potions class is harder than Charms for Neville. Hogwarts is better than the Dursleys’ house.” Corrija com cores: verde = ótimo, amarelo = revisar.

    🎙️ Part 3 — Speaking Challenge

    Record a voice message of 1–2 minutes comparing two characters or magical things (e.g. Harry 🆚 Draco, owl 🆚 broomstick). Don’t write the whole text first! Use: “Let me think…”, “I mean…”, “It’s similar to…”. If you make a mistake, keep going. 🌊

    Feedback no próximo encontro: primeiro conteúdo/coragem, depois 2 pontos de língua. Critério de sucesso: ela falou o tempo todo sem parar para perfeição.
